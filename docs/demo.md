# Demo guide

Live game: https://tjjpkxhe.idos.games/

## Two-minute walkthrough

1. Sign in through iDosGames and start the game. Point out that no tokens are
   required for entry.
2. Advance through the introduction, move around the island, gather resources,
   and show the inventory and crafting.
3. Open the shop icon next to the resource counters. Explain that the dialog
   offers a free holder bonus, not purchases.
4. In Chrome with Phantom/Solflare, confirm wallet ownership with a signed
   message. The balance query is read-only; tokens stay in the wallet.
5. If the verified main account holds at least 5,000 ESCAPE, choose one tool.
   Show it in the inventory and show that the other choices are now blocked.
6. Close with the objective: repair the boat and escape.

A recorded video is not available yet. Do not present the local test harness as
a successful human wallet login: it deliberately supplies a test linked wallet.

Controls: WASD/arrows to move, E to interact, Tab for inventory, Space to attack.
